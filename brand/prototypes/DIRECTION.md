## 2. What the references say

From Rafael's Trove (and the Are.na channels it imports: IRIS, Metamorph, grphc, Product / Light, style — optimistic) and his Cosmos collections:

1. **Engraving on one flat bright** — Cosmos *trove* (40+ saves): woodcut and engraved emblems — horses, pegasus, griffins — each alone on a single flat field: green, vermilion, pink, cobalt. This is Rueca's old/new tension at its simplest, and it's what he collects most.
2. **One crafted object, lit like a product** — Cosmos *Gold*, *White Glove Transfer*, Trove *Product / Light*: wax seals, brass bells, engraved metal, lighters on vermilion, coins on black. Old-craft objects shot with modern product lighting. Single hue backdrop, one key light, crisp contact shadow.
3. **Science plates as diagrams** — Trove *IRIS* + *Scientific Illustration* + *Technical Diagram*: Haeckel symmetry plates, engraved optics diagrams, lettered coordinate points over botanicals, compass constructions, herbarium sheets. Labels, numbers and construction lines doing the composing.
4. **Dither and stipple of classical subjects** — Cosmos *Stippling*, *technical textures*; Trove: Atkinson portraits, a dot-dithered classical bust, "Archer" engraving as blue dither, halftone screen-frequency plates.
5. **Editorial UI on paper** — Trove: didone/serif headlines over sans captions on cream and sage ("puttin in the reps"), *Dither Cards* (serif + halftone imagery in product UI), the art-book shop with spines as the list, the museum archive where only colour blocks and labels move.
6. **Flat colour blocks** — Cosmos *color palettes*: Albers squares, Suprematist blocks, riso overprint, chalky brights against paper.
7. **Hardware labelling** — Trove *Metamorph*: Teenage Engineering, Elektron, engraved instrument disks ("PRESSURE / PROXIMITY"). Dense, exact, small labels.

What he doesn't save: glossy gradients, neon-on-black, generic SaaS mockups. That's most of what the shells show today.

## 3. The idea: three clients, one studio

Every demo becomes work for one of three fictional clients — each an **old craft relaunched today**, so the old/new tension lives in the content itself, not just the frame. The gallery reads as one studio's portfolio.

| Client | What it is | Bright | Old half | New half |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gabinete** | A museum of scientific instruments and its visitor app | Cielo | Engraved instrument plates (astrolabe, armillary sphere, telescope, balance, sundial) | The app, tickets, audio guide |
| **Agalla** | Natural inks, made from galls and dyes, sold online | Achiote (+ the six dyes as the range) | Iron-gall ink, quills, wax seals | Product photography, a web shop, social formats |
| **Campana** | A bell foundry making one modern desk bell | Gualda (brass) | Cast bronze, the engraved bell plate | 3D configurator, launch film |

(Names are plain Spanish words: *gabinete* = cabinet of curiosities, *agalla* = oak gall, the source of iron-gall ink, *campana* = bell. No fabric or weaving anywhere.)

## 5. How the pictures get made

- **Engravings** (Midjourney, the Diderot `--sref` from RUECA §6, seed 4417): tested 10 Oct, the hand bell came out at the animate plate's quality first try. Post-process each to Nogal ink on transparent (`inked()`), so it themes light/dark like animate.
- **Photographs** (Midjourney `--style raw`): tested 10 Oct, the ink bottle on vermilion is at the level of the *Gold* / *White Glove* saves. One prompt skeleton for all photos (one object, seamless single-hue paper, one soft key light from the left, contact shadow, medium format) so they read as one shoot.
- **Nano Banana** (Gemini) for edits: remove backgrounds, split parts (as the animate wheel was), make the six-colour bottle set consistent from one master.
- **Procedural** where the picture must react: Chladni (synth), hachures (nodes), the bell geometry (scene).
- **Chladni plates via Midjourney: tested, rejected** (muddy, wrong patterns). Drawn in code instead.

Estimated: ~10 engravings, ~10 photos, 3 medallions, a few Nano Banana edits. All files land in `public/demo/<shell>/`, WebP, like animate.
