Naming and brand

Three rounds, 177 names, all checked against npm, GitHub, domains and the web. Round 2 went looking in Spanish and Latin, in the history of tools, among inventors and in pop culture. Round 3 went cute: lil- names, toys and fictional makers. Each shortlist has drawn marks, a wordmark and a palette. The final name and look are your call.

Your call · 1Pick the name

Overall, my pick is Cruceta, the marionette’s control bar: we don’t make your prototype, we make it move. If you want cute, it’s Trompo. Thumbwheel is still the safest English pick.

Once you pick

  1. Claim the handles the same day: the GitHub org, the npm @scope (free to create) and the domain. Free things go fast once a repo is public.
  2. Rename in one place: edit src/site.config.ts (name, slug, tagline, repo, url), then run npm run sync. The skill, plugin, registry and README follow.
  3. Swap the placeholder Mark in src/landing/parts.tsx for the chosen SVG from brand/marks.js. The product chrome stays neutral, and the brand colour lives on the site and the mark only.
  4. Get a quick trademark search (USPTO / EUIPO, class 9 and 42) before the public launch.
