Rueca: the rustic rethink

11 Oct 2026 · grounded in your Trove

Trompo is retired. Rueca is the spinning wheel that turns raw fibre into thread, and you asked for something more rustic, probably with a serif display. Before drawing anything I read about 90 of your saves across brand identity, type, textiles, palettes and craft. Seven patterns repeat, and everything below is built from them, with links back to the saves. The pictures are stand-ins, because you’re drawing the mark and imagery by hand. Palette v2: rustic meets modern, “Figma made in the 90s”

The ground and ink stay rustic: wool paper, walnut, madder oxblood, plus the platinum grey of a 1997 Mac window. The brights are Figma’s five hues and a yolk yellow, printed flat and a little chalky instead of glowing, each named after the natural dye that makes it. They’re tuned against colours sampled from ayushsoni_io and HEX’s work (Cardinal, the legal-tech system, the Klein biotech set). Left chip is light, right chip is dark.

Handwriting instead of mono

Labels, measurements, captions and red-pen notes are now handwritten. All seven options are free and open-licensed, so the open-source site can ship them. Code stays in Geist Mono, because a command people copy has to be unambiguous. Click one to apply it everywhere above, or use the Hand menu.

Once you pick

  1. Claim the handles the same day: the GitHub org, the npm @scope (free to create) and the domain. Free things go fast once a repo is public.
  2. Rename in one place: edit src/site.config.ts (name, slug, tagline, repo, url), then run npm run sync. The skill, plugin, registry and README follow.
  3. Swap the placeholder Mark in src/landing/parts.tsx for the chosen SVG from brand/marks.js. The product chrome stays neutral, and the brand colour lives on the site and the mark only.
  4. Get a quick trademark search (USPTO / EUIPO, class 9 and 42) before the public launch.
