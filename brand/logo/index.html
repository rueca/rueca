Your two-wheel mark measured and rebuilt, then nine ideas around it: two that stay close, four twists, and three that start from somewhere else. Each one is drawn from circles and tangents, so you can switch on its construction.

The wordRueca: wheel, distaff, twistRAE gives three senses: the distaff, the spinning wheel, and "a turn or twist of something". All three are fair game.

ConstructionNotes

The idea holds up: a drive wheel, a whorl and the band between them, with the bottoms level so the band's lower run is a baseline. Your top tangent is already true to within 0.1°. The polish is in four small places:

  1. The whorl sits high. It's 2.4 units above its loop's centre, so the gap round it runs from 18.4 to 23.2. It reads as rolling uphill.
  2. Two gaps, no rule. 23.1 round the wheel, about 20.8 round the whorl. A small shape wants a slightly smaller gap, but it should be a decision: the rebuild uses 22.5 / 19.5.
  3. A soft joint. Measured against true arcs, the outline is within 0.1 units on average, but it drifts up to 1.1 where the top run meets the whorl's loop, so the band runs about 24–26 there. You can't see it at 24px; at hero size you can. As one stroked path, it's exactly 25.
  4. No small cut. At 16px the band is 1.8px and the whorl 3px. A second drawing for small sizes (band 34, gaps 19 / 15) keeps the whorl visible in a favicon.
