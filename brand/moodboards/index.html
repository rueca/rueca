Where I’d go

These scores are my opinion out of five, there to start the conversation, not to settle it.

Start from Lámina técnica, and borrow Patrón’s motion.

Lámina is the elegant form of the metaphor. The wheel shows up as knowledge, an exact labelled drawing, rather than as a mascot. It extends the Grabado direction you chose, so nothing we’ve built gets thrown away. It also gives you a clear drawing job: three plates.

On its own, Lámina is a bit still. Patrón’s Achiote thread and slowly turning rings are already in the WheelField hero, so carry them over for the homepage and keep the plates for everything else.

Platino 97 is the bet I’d take if you want people to smile. It’s the most literal take on “Figma made in the 90s”, and the Platino colour is already named after it. Its window chrome also works as a frame for shell screenshots, whichever board wins.

Where your eye matters

  1. Serious or playful? Lámina and Archivo whisper. Platino winks. Choosing between them is mostly choosing a tone.
  2. Will you draw or photograph? Lámina, Patrón and Platino are drawing jobs. Materia needs a shoot or 3D.
  3. How much engraving do you want? Every board uses engravings as references. Decide whether your own drawings should look engraved (hatching) or stay pure line.
  4. Logo colours: settled. The palette tokens win, the logo is a work in progress, and its file now uses Gualda, Cielo and Achiote.

Once you pick, I’ll write the visual brief: every drawing or photo to make, with sizes, where each one goes, and the rules it follows.
