# Nomenclature of Rueca's colours [proposed]

A draft (16 Oct 2026, from research/28-brand-soul-audit.md, §3). Every colour in Mora gets an entry, after Werner's *Nomenclature of Colours* (1821): where it comes from, what part of it dyes, and the job it does in Rueca. Five are real dyes, two are materials, and two are images whose dye is named honestly. The hex values are RUECA.md's; nothing here changes them.

Voice: like a dyer's notebook. One fact per line, short, no adjectives that a swatch can show.

| Name | English | Source (Latin) | Part | Note | Job in Rueca |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Achiote** | annatto | *Bixa orellana* | the seeds' red coat | Ground for centuries in Mesoamerica, for cloth, food and skin. It still colours Red Leicester. | The lead colour: the call to action, the red pen, the punchline. |
| **Gualda** | weld, dyer's rocket | *Reseda luteola* | the whole plant, cut in flower | Europe's steadiest yellow; it holds where others fade. | Accent. |
| **Hoja** | leaf green | Gualda over Añil | two dips: yellow over blue | Nature has no fast green. Dyers made it from yellow over blue. | Accent. |
| **Cielo** | sky blue | dyed with añil, *Indigofera suffruticosa* | the leaves, fermented in a vat | Dyed with añil, the indigo of the Americas. | Accent; the tools' blue. |
| **Malva** | mallow | *Malva sylvestris* | the flower | *Mauve* is French for mallow. The colour kept the plant's name. | Accent; the hero's loop. |
| **Mora** | blackberry, mulberry | *Rubus ulmifolius*, *Morus nigra* | the fruit | More a stain than a dye: it runs. We keep it for depth. | Achiote's shadow; the deep colour. |
| **Nogal** | walnut | *Juglans regia* | the green hull | Walnut-hull ink: the drafter's brown-black. | Ink. All drawing, all text. |
| **Lana** | wool | *Ovis aries* | the fleece | What the wheel is for. | Paper. |
| **Platino** | platinum | the metal | — | The one colour that isn't grown. | The grey plate. |

## How it's used

- **The dye specimen** (`brand/specimen/`): one entry per plate, after Werner. It has the swatch, the name and source, the source engraved, and the job written in hand. It's also a bento tile that turns to the next dye on each beat.
- **Captions:** wherever a colour is named in brand pieces (palette tiles, the brand book, merch tags), use the Spanish name, then the source in italics: *Achiote, Bixa orellana*.
- **Facts, not flavour:** if a line can't be checked, it doesn't go in.
