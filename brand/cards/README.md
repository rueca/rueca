# Cards

Rueca's share images, made with a Rueca tool. Open it with the `brand-board` dev server at `/brand/cards/`.

## Templates

- **Headline**: the lockup, the site's line in Goudy, and the site's pen on it: a Malva loop round one word and an Achiote line under another. The dyes run in reading order along the foot. This is the share image (`renders/og-v2.jpg`, 1200 × 630), replacing the walnut-era `public/og-v1.jpg`.
- **Release**: a numbered *Lámina* per version. It has the version's spinning word, the engraving of that word, the meaning and a note in the hand. 0.2 is *Malacate* (the spindle whorl). The version words are a proposal (research/28, device 4).
- **Dye**: one colour from the nomenclature (`brand/specimen/entries.ts`): swatch, engraving, name, Latin, fact and job.

The formats are link preview 1200 × 630, square, 4:5, story 9:16 and a 3:1 profile header. Every layout is worked out from the card's own size. The headline is set as large as fits, in balanced lines.

The grounds are the fields Mora allows: Lana, Platino, Nogal, Mora and Achiote. Brights stay blocks and pens, never fields.

Export the PNG at the card's size, or a short video of the pen drawing on.
