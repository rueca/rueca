# The hand and the engraving: references, and a cleaner Midjourney recipe

16 Oct 2026. Rafael asked for references from brands with a hand-drawn element like ours, and for more polished Midjourney renders. Rueca has two hand layers:
- **the pen**: La Belle Aurore in Achiote, for margin notes, circles, underlines and spec redlines;
- **the plates**: copperplate engravings from Midjourney, inked to Nogal.

## What we found

- **No tech brand runs engraving as a system.** The ground is open, but the craft lessons have to come from print: hedcuts, scratchboard, packaging.
- **The handwriting brands that work give the hand rules:**
  - one colour;
  - one job: intent, opinion, "why";
  - one zone: marketing and specimens, not product chrome;
  - a pace.
- **Our engravings are good one at a time but don't read as one set.** The fix is a house style reference made from our own clean outputs, plus a stricter prompt. See the before and after below.

## Rules for the hand (proposed)

1. **One colour**: Achiote. Malva only for the headline loop, as on the site.
2. **One job**: the hand says *why*, or points. It never sets a headline or body copy.
3. **One zone**: landing, brand pieces, specimens and the bento. Never the kit's UI chrome.
4. **One mark at a time**, about a second each, drawn rather than faded (RUECA §7, rule 1).
5. **A size floor** of 13 px, and a heavier stroke where it's small.

## What's off in our engravings

Laid side by side (`before-after.jpg`, top row), each one is fine but they don't match:
- **Tone instead of line.** The achiote pods go solid black, and the mallow leaves read like a filtered photo. RUECA §8 says "lines stay lines".
- **Different hands.** Hatching density and line weight change from plate to plate.
- **Fake lettering.** Stray engraved letters (A, B, Fig.) survive the crop.
- **Paper and borders.** The aged paper tone and the plate rules have to be cut and keyed out by hand for every image, and sometimes colour leaks in (a red achiote pod).
- **The Wikimedia `--sref` often fails to load**, so the reference changes from batch to batch.

## The recipe, tested

**Three rounds:** achiote, mallow and walnut, 40 images (`round-3-tests.jpg` is the last round).

- **A stricter prompt with no style reference** gives clean white ground and open line, but drifts towards stock clip-art engraving.
- **Our old engraving as the reference** brings back the yellow paper and the heavy tone.
- **Two of our own clean outputs as a combined reference ("house")** held one family across all three plants: white ground, open hatching, one line weight, no letters, no colour.

**Proposed recipe (RUECA §6 v2):**

```
<subject, named parts: "a sprig of X, Latin name: two leaves, three pods, one split open…">,
botanical copperplate line engraving, burin lines only, shading built from fine parallel hatching
and light cross-hatching, open even line spacing, crisp black lines on plain white paper, one
isolated specimen centred with generous margins, light from the upper left, no lettering, no
labels, no frame
--no text, letters, numbers, border, frame, paper texture, stains, grey wash, solid black areas, cast shadow, colour, photograph
--ar 4:5 --seed 4417 --style raw --stylize 50
--sref https://cdn.midjourney.com/81cd0bf3-ac5a-4a12-8b29-629ac8138875/0_2.png https://cdn.midjourney.com/0606d648-6f78-4724-99ac-9d02877f68bb/0_0.png --sw 100
```

**Post-process:** `python3 brand/tools/ink.py in.png out.webp --w 900 --crop 0.02,0.02,0.98,0.98 --sides 0.01`. No border hunting is needed, then clear alpha below 34.

**What it costs:** the plates lose some of the Diderot grit and read a little more modern. If Rafael wants more age back, raise `--sw` towards 200, or swap one of the two references for a real Diderot plate re-hosted on Midjourney (Wikimedia links don't load reliably).
