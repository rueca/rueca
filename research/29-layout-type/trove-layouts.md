# Trove layouts: grids and pages for "editorial meets SaaS"

Collected 16 Oct 2026 from Rafael's Trove through the x-book tools only. Aim: give Rueca's landing page and docs a stronger grid, somewhere between a magazine and a SaaS site.

## How this was gathered, and what it can and cannot tell us

- Tags browsed: landing-pages, portfolio-sites, editorial-design, type-specimen, brand-guidelines, grid-systems, ui-design, poster-design. Plain-language searches: editorial website grid with large serif headlines, SaaS landing page that feels like a magazine, asymmetric column layout, footnotes and margin notes, specimen page layout, bento grid, documentation site with editorial typography, plus follow-ups on footers, numbered sections, index tables, engravings in layouts, hand annotations, long reads and crosshair heroes.
- **The Trove is strong on print and weak on real web layouts.** Almost everything useful sits under grid-systems and editorial-design, and most of it is a book spread, catalogue or poster saved from Are.na or Pinterest. The landing-pages tag is mostly brand work and effects, not page structure. That suits Rueca (the brand is a printed sheet), but it means the web moves below are translations from print, and they should be tried in the browser before being trusted.
- Every line below comes from Rafael's own saved descriptions. I have not opened the live pages. Where a URL is an Are.na or Pinterest block, it is the page Rafael saved, which may be a repost of the original work.
- Tags used: **Web** is a live site or a screen design. **Print** is a book, poster or catalogue.
- Rafael left almost no notes of his own on these saves, so there is no "he said it was good" signal to lean on. The picks are mine.

Count: 42 references in 8 patterns, then 8 layout moves.

## 1. Magazine-style hero

**Editorial journal layout with extreme negative space** (Print). https://www.are.na/block/20389177
- Does: wide-tracked micro caps, a huge empty field and a three-up photo grid pushed to the lower right.

## Eight layout moves for Rueca

Current state, for comparison (from `src/landing/plate.tsx`, `plate.css` and `Landing.tsx`): the sheet is at most 1128px wide with 12px side margins on phones and 48px above 640px, the hero is centred in an 820px block, the kit grid is 2 then 3 columns, and the splits are 5 to 7. These moves build on that, not replace it.

1. **Put a 12-column grid under the sheet.** Keep the 1128px sheet. Use 12 columns with 24px gutters, and 48px outer margin (24px at tablet, 12px on phones as now). Draw hairline rules only at columns 3, 6 and 9, so the 3 and 4 column splits both land on a rule. Set a 24px baseline for vertical rhythm. Add a "G" key that draws all twelve columns in Achiote, as in the printed-grid book spreads (section 6). Sources: Tondreau, the four-column captions spread, the coffee packaging grid.

2. **Make the hero asymmetric from 1024px up.** Headline in Goudy across columns 1 to 8, left-aligned. Lede and install command in columns 1 to 5. The hero plate sits lower right across columns 7 to 12 and may overlap the headline's last line by one gutter. A small mono marker pair sits in the sheet corners. Keep the centred hero below 1024px, where there is no room to be asymmetric. Sources: the negative-space journal layout, Brisbane, RRE, Exa. This is the biggest visual change and Rafael should see it in the browser before it ships.

3. **Give reading pages a three-part layout: rail, text, margin.** Docs, FAQ and essays use columns 1 to 2 for a rail (section numeral, sheet reference, small caps), columns 3 to 8 for text, and columns 9 to 12 for the margin. The measure is 62 to 68 characters, about 640px at 17px, which matches the `max-w-[640px]` already used for body copy. Below 1024px the margin notes drop under their paragraph, indented, with a short pen rule. Sources: Maggie Appleton, the Abloh footnote spread, the Max Joseph columns.

4. **Hand notes live in the right margin, and in the text only as marks.** At most two pen notes per screen, each 12 words or fewer, set in the margin column with a leader line in the measure pen to the word they refer to, tilted one or two degrees. Inside paragraphs the pen may only underline, strike or circle, as in the Polidano spread. Never put a pen note in body copy, buttons or the nav. Sources: the herbarium sheet, Polidano, the Acconci record.

5. **Snap plates to column spans, and put the caption in the margin.** Plates are 4, 6 or 8 columns wide, double-ruled, with "Fig. n" in the margin column beside them on wide screens and under them on narrow ones. One plate per page may break its frame by one gutter at a single corner. A faded engraving at about 10 to 12 percent ink may sit behind a section heading. An isometric kit drawing sits on one flat bright block spanning 6 columns, and no more than one bright block is in view at a time. Sources: the Hoya plate, the ghosted-engraving book spreads, the VR article blocks.

6. **Turn the shells into an index table.** Rows 56px high on a rule, columns: large Goudy numeral, name, one line of what it does, status, arrow. The hover bar runs the full row width in a flat bright. Use it for the twelve shells and the kit list, and add a small spec table beside each plate. Sources: FP01, MOAA, the travel adaptor spec table.

7. **Make the bento tiles share rules, not gaps.** Tiles span 4, 6 or 8 of the 12 columns, in heights that are multiples of the 24px baseline, with shared hairline borders and no rounded corners, as the current `.cells` grid already does. Fill tiles with engraved plates on Lana, one flat bright tile per row at most, and serif copy on the tile. Sources: the @saintdsgn banknote bento, the coffee packaging system, the cabinet wall.

8. **Turn the footer into a colophon.** Four columns of 3 grid columns each in small mono caps: licence and version, typefaces, where to find it, contributors. Above it, one Achiote block carries the contributors and stars list in tight type. The wordmark is small and sits bottom right, not huge. The existing title block stays as the frame. Sources: the colophon page, the backer-credits spread, Kurppa Hosk, RRE.

## Gaps and next steps

- The Trove has no clear example of a **SaaS pricing or comparison table** in an editorial style, and none of a **documentation site** with real editorial typography. If those matter, a few web saves are worth adding (Stripe Press, Linear's changelog, Are.na's own site, Read.cv-style pages), or I can look them up on the open web in a separate pass.
- Nothing in here has been tested at phone width. Moves 2, 3 and 5 depend on how the margin column collapses, so they should be prototyped on the real landing page before being adopted.
- The brand file still names IBM Plex as the reading face (`brand/RUECA.md`), while the current direction says Host Grotesk. That mismatch is worth settling before the specimen page is built.
