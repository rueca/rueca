# One-tone styles and the poster series

16 Oct 2026. Rafael asked for Rueca's generative art rendered in about twelve historical styles that work in one tone, a poster series in Figma, and a comparison with top SaaS companies and design studios.

## The research

- `history.md`: 30 one-tone styles, from copperplate to cyanotype. Each has its origin, signature, the tells that make it look fake, a canvas algorithm and a fit rating, ending in a ranked twelve.
- `peers.md`: HEX in depth, plus about 30 studios and SaaS brands. It ends with ten checks the posters had to clear, and three compositions to borrow.
- `srefs.md`: Midjourney style references for 20 styles, and how to make a house reference.
- `trove.md`: 110 saves from Rafael's Trove in 12 families, and 5 poster layouts drawn from them.

## How the series changed

- **v1–v3:** each style had its own source and crop (photos, the Saxony plate, the mark). See `posters-v3/`.
- **The critique:** a catalogue of looks (HEX's warning), photos reading as filters, and type that never touches the image.
- **v4–v5, "Una rueda, doce manos":**
  - the mark is carved as a relief from its own geometry
  - one crop, twelve renderers
  - the title sits up against the drawing
  - the hand goes inside the plate with a leader
  - each sheet prints its settings
  - one ink per sheet

## Where it lives

- The tool is `brand/styles/`, with renders in `brand/styles/renders/`.
- The Figma page is "Una rueda, doce manos" in Rueca Branding: twelve editable sheets.
