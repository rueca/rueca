# Critique of the v3 posters (art director's pass)

16 Oct 2026. An art director's review of `posters-v3/` against the ten checks in `peers.md` and real work (HEX First Star, Exa and Atomic Tessellator; Supabase Launch Week; Koto's Stack Overflow; Config; Vercel; Linear; DIA; Mucho; Dumbar; Pentagram). This summary is from the reviewer's report. What was changed in v4 and v5 is at the end.

## Biggest gaps against the field

1. Type and image never touch. First Star, Exa and Supabase put the headline in the field.
2. The renders read as filtered photos. Source from the engraved plate and the logo's geometry instead.
3. No tool behind the images. Config, DIA and Koto each run one system that states the product idea. Print each sheet's parameters.

## The proposed angle

**"One wheel, twelve hands":** the same subject at the same crop through all twelve renderers, like a type specimen, with each sheet printing its settings. Every poster then proves the tagline: a prototype spun into a tunable tool.

## What v4 and v5 changed

- **One subject:** the mark, carved as a relief from its exact geometry. No photos.
- **Crop:** one crop for all twelve, bleeding off both sides.
- **Title:** Goudy at up to 168 px, set against the drawing.
- **Three type sizes:** Goudy, Plex 15/17 and Plex caps 12/13. Achiote and Cielo get the larger pair for contrast.
- **Hand note:** inside the plate, with a leader to the band.
- **Settings line:** each renderer reports the numbers it used, in place of prose.
- **Per-style fixes:**
  - copperplate hatching follows the form
  - wood engraving is white line on a solid block
  - riso has solids plus grain
  - dither uses an 8 px pixel
  - ASCII uses a 10-step ramp measured from the font
  - the plotter follows Nees on the grid
  - contours are smoothed
  - the cyanotype has a straight plate
- **Not done yet:**
  - a paper proof (check 10)
  - the reviewer's second layout variant, the "prototype and tool" diptych
  - sources from the Saxony plate, which only the Sketchbook view offers
